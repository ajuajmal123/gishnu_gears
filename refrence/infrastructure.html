ISO 9001:2015 Quality Standards

Every gearbox manufactured at Gishnu Gears passes through a rigorous multi-stage quality inspection process from raw material verification to final testing before shipment.

STEP 1

Research & Development

Customer requirements analyzed, gear ratios calculated, technical drawings finalized, with prototype development where required.

STEP 2

Material Procurement

Certified raw materials sourced from approved vendors to meet mechanical and metallurgical specifications.

STEP 3

CNC Precision Machining

Gear cutting, hobbing, shaping, and grinding on CNC and precision machines to achieve tight tolerances.

STEP 4

Special Processes

Heat treatment, surface hardening, and protective finishes such as powder coating as per application needs.

STEP 5

Gearbox Assembly

Careful assembly of gears, shafts, housings, and bearings in controlled conditions.

STEP 6

Gearbox Testing

Load, noise, backlash, and efficiency tests performed on dedicated test rigs.

STEP 7

Final QA

Dimensional checks, documentation review, and ISO 9001:2015 verification before release.

STEP 8

Packing & Dispatch

Export-standard packing and safe delivery to customers worldwide.
